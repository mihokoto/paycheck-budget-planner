# Paycheck Budget Planner

A budget planner that runs on pay periods instead of calendar months. It shows what is safe to spend until your next payday, sets money aside for bills that land every few months, keeps spending within simple limits, tracks savings goals, and plans debt payoff. It runs in the browser with no account, and everything you enter stays on your device.

**Try the demo:** https://mihokoto.github.io/paycheck-budget-planner/
